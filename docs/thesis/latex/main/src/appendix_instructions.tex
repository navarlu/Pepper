\chapter{Visitor Instruction Board}
\label{ch:instructions}

This appendix reproduces the printed instruction board placed next to Pepper at the reception desk during the experiment.
The board was the only briefing visitors received under the original passive-recruitment plan (\secref{sec:exp_visitor_flow}) and explained what Pepper can help with, how to talk to her, and how to fill in the post-interaction questionnaire.
