\chapter{Visitor Instruction Board}
\label{ch:instructions}

This appendix reproduces the printed instruction board placed next to Pepper at the reception desk during the field study.
The board was the only briefing visitors received under the original passive-recruitment plan (\secref{sec:exp_visitor_flow}) and explained, on a single page, what Pepper can help with, how to talk to her, and how to fill in the post-interaction questionnaire.
