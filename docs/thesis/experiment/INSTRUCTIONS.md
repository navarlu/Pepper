# Meet Pepper — Receptionist

**Part of a research experiment for the diploma thesis:**
*Design of an LLM-Driven Receptionist Robot for Social Interaction*

---

## Informed Consent — Please Read

By speaking into the microphone, you agree that your **audio recording may be stored and used** for this thesis research and its evaluation.

If you do not consent, please do not speak to Pepper.

---

## What Pepper Can Help You With

- **Navigation** — directions to rooms in the building (please use the **room code**, e.g. E-107)
- **Lecture schedule** — finding the time and place of a lecture (please use the **subject code**, e.g. HRO)
- **Staff contacts** — finding contact details for faculty members and staff
  *(note: Czech names may sometimes be misrecognised — try spelling or repeating)*
- **Mensa menu** — today's menu in the canteen
- **General conversation** — feel free to just chat with her

**Language:** Pepper currently speaks **English only.**

---

## How to Talk to Her

1. **Watch the tablet** — it shows Pepper's current state
2. **Speak when she is listening** — at all other times the microphone is muted
3. **Prepare your question first**, then say it **clearly and without long pauses**
4. The conversation **resets automatically** after a short period of inactivity
5. **Note your conversation ID** from the tablet — you'll be asked for it in the questionnaire

## After Your Interaction

It would help my research enormously if you could fill in a **short 3-minute questionnaire** about your experience — the results will be used to evaluate the interaction quality.

> ⚠️ **Before you leave:** make sure you have your **conversation ID** from Pepper's tablet — the questionnaire requires it to link your answers to the interaction.
